THE MAN BEHIND THE HAMMER

An introduction to the Tribune's most feared investigative journalist.

FRANK “THE HAMMER” HAGGERTY

Investigative Journalist. Professional Pain in the Ass. Enemy of Bullshit.

Nobody knows exactly where Frank Haggerty came from.

And the few people who claim they do generally stop talking when his name comes up.

What is known is that Frank has spent a considerable portion of his miserable existence digging into things other people would prefer remained buried.

Political scandals. Crooked businessmen. Shady backroom dealings. Powerful men with expensive lawyers and suspiciously clean reputations.

Frank has investigated them all.

Or so the stories go.

His employment history is a collection of unanswered questions, missing paperwork and former colleagues who suddenly remember having somewhere else to be.

Ask Frank about his past and he’ll tell you to mind your own goddamn business.

Ask him twice and he’ll probably throw you out of his office.

But there’s one thing nobody disputes.

Frank Haggerty cannot be bought.

Not with money. Not with threats. Not with promises of power, influence or a lifetime supply of top-shelf whiskey.

Frank has no allegiance to any person, organization, newspaper or institution.

His only loyalty is to the truth.

And he’ll drag that truth into the daylight kicking and screaming if he has to.

Unfortunately for the fantasy football community, Frank has turned his attention toward a particularly disturbing collection of individuals: fantasy football managers.

Men and women entrusted with draft picks, waiver budgets and starting lineups who routinely demonstrate the decision-making abilities of a drunken raccoon trapped inside a casino.

Frank considers this an epidemic.

He intends to expose it.

Every catastrophic trade. Every inexplicable benching. Every outrageous waiver bid. Every manager who insists they knew what they were doing despite overwhelming evidence to the contrary.

But Frank isn’t interested in manufacturing scandals.

When a manager makes a brilliant decision, Frank will report that too. He may dislike the individual, question their intelligence and insult their haircut, but he’ll give them the credit they’ve earned.

Because the facts are the facts.

His language is atrocious. His drinking habits are concerning. His temper has been described as a workplace hazard.

His journalism, however, is beyond reproach.

As for the nickname The Hammer?

Nobody seems to agree on where it came from.

Frank isn’t particularly interested in clearing that up.

THE HAMMER'S CODE · RULE NO. 1

EVERY MANAGER DESERVES A FAIR INVESTIGATION.

“Unfortunately, most of you stupid bastards keep providing evidence against yourselves.”

“I don't give a fuck who you are. I give a fuck what's true.”— Frank “The Hammer” Haggerty
