THE FOURTH & FUCKUP TRIBUNE · READER SERVICES

PRIVACY POLICY

The short version: We use reader information to operate The Fourth & Fuckup Tribune, respond to messages, and manage subscriptions. We do not sell reader information.

Information We Collect

When you contact the newsroom, we collect the email address, name (if provided), topic, subject, and message you submit. Your message may contain other details you choose to share. If you subscribe to email updates, we collect the email address needed to manage that subscription. If you use reader account or comment features, information you provide through those features may also be processed.

How We Use It

We use this information to respond to inquiries, review corrections and feedback, manage email subscriptions, operate reader features, and protect the website from abuse. Please do not include sensitive personal information in messages.

Who Handles the Information

Authorized Tribune editors can review newsroom correspondence. We use third-party service providers to operate website features, including Supabase for storing newsroom messages, Cloudflare Turnstile for form security, and Brevo for newsroom email notifications. These services process information as needed to provide their functions. We do not sell reader information or share it with advertisers.

Newsroom Notifications

When a newsroom message is submitted, an email alert may be sent to the Tribune. The alert identifies the reader's name (if provided), topic, and subject, but does not include the full message or the reader's email address. The full submission remains in the private newsroom inbox.

How Long We Keep It

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to manage correspondence, subscriptions, and website operations. We have not established an automatic deletion schedule for newsroom messages. You can request deletion at any time using the contact address below, subject to any legal or operational need to retain particular records.

Your Choices and Requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you submitted to the Tribune. You can also ask to stop receiving subscription emails. Write to fourthandfuckup@gmail.com. We may need to verify that a request relates to your information before acting on it.

Security and Updates

We use access controls and anti-abuse checks to help protect reader information, although no online system can guarantee complete security. We may update this policy as Tribune features change; the current version will appear on this page.
